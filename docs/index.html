What v0.8.2 fixed

Live diagnostics showed that many huge marginal edges had almost no finite depth. Canonical V4Quoter frequently returned NotEnoughLiquidity(poolId). One observed route could quote $0.01 with positive gross edge, but $0.03 and above failed and the $0.01 paper net remained negative after gas, allowance and safety costs.

The engine now probes from $0.01 and follows an ascending exact-depth ladder. A decoded NotEnoughLiquidity becomes an observed capacity boundary; smaller successful quotes are retained and larger sizes are not attempted. Unknown errors still fail closed.

Dashboard

Measurement boundary

V4Quoter gas remains a research proxy rather than deployed atomic-executor gas. Quote completion is not transaction inclusion. A positive sample would be paper evidence, not realized profit.
